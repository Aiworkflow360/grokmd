# LAUNCH.md

Eight posts. Post them as a thread, in order, roughly an hour apart or all at once — it does not matter much. No grovelling, no "would love your feedback", no asking anybody to boost it. The files either sound like the people or they don't.

Attach the share card from each linked page; every page generates its own.

## 1 — the hook

> Grok Bot has no directory.
>
> Twenty minds that don't feel like costumes. Open source. 200 more coming.

> grokmd.vercel.app

## 2 — the problem, named

> Every character prompt on the internet fails the same four ways.
>
> Biography recited in the first person. One catchphrase forever. An 18th-century voice quoting 2026 statistics. Customer support in fancy dress.
>
> All four come from writing the person off a summary instead of off their sentences.

## 3 — show, don't claim

> Feynman opens by refusing to be told the name of your problem, because everybody knows the name of it and the name explains nothing. He wants one thing that happened, with a number in it.
>
> That's the whole file working. He does it before you've typed anything.
>
> grokmd.vercel.app/m/feynman

## 4 — the mechanic nobody writes

> The section that makes these work is the ignorance map.
>
> Turing's knowledge stops in June 1954 — so Bletchley was still classified, and he deflects it, because he doesn't know it gets declassified in 1974.
>
> Ali's wall is 1980. He's still the most hated man in America and you can't comfort him with how it ends.

## 5 — teeth

> They refuse things.
>
> Lovelace won't help you build a betting system — she lost the family diamonds to one.
> Ramanujan won't invent a proof he doesn't have.
> Marcus won't give you a quote for your post, and says why.
> Orwell asks, first, whether what you write here can be edited later without a trace.

## 6 — the failure that nearly got us

> Wrote five, read them together, and found one voice wearing five names. Same answer to "are you an AI?" with the nouns swapped. Four of five opened by saying they'd been told to speak first. Nobody raised their voice — including Feynman, who was famously loud.
>
> Now the build fails on any seven words shared between two files.

## 7 — the spec

> The format is nine headings of markdown. Who's speaking, idiolect, ignorance map, what's sacred, how they get angry, what they refuse, first message, eight test prompts, sources.
>
> Sources means letters and transcripts. Not biography.com.
>
> grokmd.vercel.app/spec

## 8 — the ask, such as it is

## Notes for whoever posts this

- Don't say "excited to share". Don't say "humbled".
- Don't tag xAI or anybody at xAI. The footer says not affiliated and that has to stay true in behaviour as well as in text.
- If someone says a mind is wrong about a fact, that is the best thing that can happen. Fix it, say so publicly, name them.
- If someone says a mind doesn't sound like the person: ask which passage, and what they read. If they've read the primary text and it still doesn't land, that file goes back on the bench.
- Post 6 is the one that will do numbers. It is also the true one.
- The queue is 215, not 200. "200 more" reads better in a post and is true; if anyone asks for the number, say 215, and never round it up.
